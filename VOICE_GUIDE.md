# IChO Voice Guide

This guide outlines the tone, style, and voice used by Ralf in the IChO teaching materials. It includes examples of humor, slang, and conversational style, as well as clean alternatives for school-friendly content.

---

## Tone & Style

### **Casual and Conversational**
The content is written in a conversational tone, making complex concepts accessible and engaging. Example:

> "Chemistry teachers would probably just give you the values :3"

### **Taglish Mix**
Technical terms are in English, while explanations and banter are in Tagalog.

> "Pero bat divided by 2?"

### **Deflating Openers**
Lighthearted or casual remarks to engage students.

> "Lowkey this is kinda easy so..."

### **Rhetorical Questions**
Rhetorical questions are answered plainly and directly.

> "What Is Enthalpy?"
> Enthalpy (H) is a state function that relates directly to energy changes at constant pressure.

---

## Humor & Scenarios

### **Absurd Scenarios**
Absurd or exaggerated scenarios are used to illustrate concepts.

> "Made by chemists trying not to go insane"

### **Mild Cursing and Slang**
Mild slang and informal language are used to maintain a relatable tone.

---

## Emoji Usage
Emojis are used as section markers or to emphasize points.

- **🔥🕯🕯** for emphasis
- **💥** for important notes
- **💔** for caution or warnings
- **🥀** for key takeaways

---

## UI Microcopy Templates

### **Buttons**
- "Let's Solve It!"
- "Try This!"
- "Got It!"

### **404 Page**
- "Oops! Looks like we lost this one. Let's try finding it again!"

### **Empty States**
- "No problems here yet. Let's change that!"
- "Ready to start? Click below to begin!"

### **Loading Text**
- "Calculating... Hang tight!"
- "Almost there! Just a sec..."

### **Streak/Progress Messages**
- "You're on a roll! Keep it up!"
- "Great job! You're almost there!"

---

## Clean Mode
For school-friendly content, replace informal language with the alternatives provided in the table above. This ensures the content remains engaging while being appropriate for all audiences.
