## Biomolecules: Ang Building Blocks ng Buhay

Biochemistry isn't just "biology." Sa IChO, it’s physical chemistry applied to biological systems. We look at the thermodynamics of protein folding, enzyme kinetics (Michaelis-Menten), and the acid-base chemistry of amino acids. 

## 1. Amino Acids: The Amphoteric Stars

Amino acids are the building blocks of proteins. They are **amphoteric** (pwedeng maging acid or base).

- **Zwitterion**: The state where the molecule has both positive and negative charges (net charge = 0).
- **Isoelectric Point (pI)**: pH kung saan ang amino acid ay nasa zwitterionic state.
  - Calculation: $pI = \frac{pK_{a1} + pK_{a2}}{2}$ (for neutral amino acids).

## 2. Proteins: Higher-Order Structure

- **Primary**: Sequence of amino acids.
- **Secondary**: $\alpha$-helices and $\beta$-sheets, maintained by **Hydrogen bonding**.
- **Tertiary**: 3D folding, maintained by hydrophobic interactions, disulfide bonds (covalent!), and salt bridges.
- **Quaternary**: Multi-subunit assemblies.

## 3. Enzyme Kinetics (Michaelis-Menten)

How fast do enzymes work?
$$v = \frac{V_{max} [S]}{K_m + [S]}$$
- $K_m$: Substrate concentration where $v = 1/2 V_{max}$. Shows affinity!
- Lineweaver-Burk Plot: Linearization para makuha yung $V_{max}$ and $K_m$ easily:
  $$\frac{1}{v} = \frac{K_m}{V_{max}} \frac{1}{[S]} + \frac{1}{V_{max}}$$

## Level-up Quiz

1. **pI Calculation**: Given $pK_{a1}=2.3, pK_{a2}=9.6$, what is the pI of this amino acid?
   *Answer: $(2.3 + 9.6) / 2 = 5.95$.*

2. **Kinetic Interpretation**: What does a high $K_m$ mean?
   *Answer: Low affinity of the enzyme for the substrate.*
