# Brief for Unit 5: Biochemistry, Lesson 1

## Title
Amino Acids, Proteins, and Enzyme Kinetics

## Goal
Teach students the structure and classification of the 20 standard amino acids, the hierarchical levels of protein structure (primary through quaternary), and the quantitative principles of enzyme kinetics (Michaelis-Menten, Lineweaver-Burk, inhibition types, and catalytic efficiency).

## Syllabus Coverage
- **Amino Acids**: Structure of the general amino acid (α-carbon, amino group, carboxyl group, R-group). Classification by side-chain properties: nonpolar aliphatic, nonpolar aromatic, polar uncharged, negatively charged (acidic), positively charged (basic). Three-letter and one-letter codes. Zwitterions, isoelectric point (pI), and peptide bond formation.
- **Proteins**: Primary structure (sequence), secondary structure (α-helices, β-sheets, turns), tertiary structure (hydrophobic collapse, hydrogen bonding, disulfide bridges, salt bridges), quaternary structure (subunit assembly). Protein folding and denaturation. Domains and motifs.
- **Enzyme Kinetics**: Michaelis-Menten equation, steady-state assumption, \(K_m\) and \(V_{max}\) definitions, turnover number \(k_{cat}\), catalytic efficiency \(k_{cat}/K_m\). Lineweaver-Burk (double-reciprocal) plots. Enzyme inhibition: competitive, noncompetitive, uncompetitive, mixed. Allosteric regulation and cooperativity (Hill equation).
- **Factors Affecting Enzyme Activity**: pH, temperature, substrate concentration, and inhibitors.

## Difficulty
Intermediate-Advanced (IChO level).

## Format (Follow STYLE_GUIDE.md)
- **Voice**: Taglish ("Ano ba naman ang amino acid na 'yan?", "Lowkey confusing yung K_m pero logic lang 'yan").
- **Header**: Standard metadata (Ralf as author, indigo/emerald color theme).
- **Terms and Definitions box**: Amino acid, zwitterion, pI, peptide bond, Michaelis-Menten equation, \(K_m\), \(V_{max}\), \(k_{cat}\), competitive/noncompetitive/uncompetitive inhibition, Lineweaver-Burk plot.
- **Worked Examples**:
    - Calculate the pI of an amino acid given its pKa values.
    - Determine \(K_m\) and \(V_{max}\) from a Lineweaver-Burk plot.
    - Predict the effect of a competitive inhibitor on apparent \(K_m\) and \(V_{max}\).
- **Callouts**:
    - **Trap**: \(K_m\) is not always equal to \(K_d\) (the dissociation constant).
    - **Graders want**: Explicit statement of the steady-state assumption and correct units (e.g., \(K_m\) in mM, \(V_{max}\) in μM/s).
- **2+ Interactives**:
    - **pI Calculator**: Input pKa values, get the isoelectric point.
    - **Lineweaver-Burk Plotter**: Adjust substrate concentration and inhibitor type, observe changes in the plot.

## Required Problems
1. **Warm-up**: Classify the 20 standard amino acids by side-chain properties.
2. **Level-up**: Calculate the pI of a tripeptide given its pKa values.
3. **Boss Fight**: Given initial velocity data at various substrate concentrations and inhibitor concentrations, determine \(K_m\), \(V_{max}\), and the inhibition type.
