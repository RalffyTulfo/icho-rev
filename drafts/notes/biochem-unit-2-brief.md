# Biochemistry Unit 1 Lesson 2: Carbohydrates, Lipids, Nucleic Acids, and Metabolism

## Goal
Provide a comprehensive, IChO-aligned introduction to the structure, function, and metabolic roles of carbohydrates, lipids, nucleic acids, and metabolism. Focus on biochemical pathways, cellular integration, and problem-solving with Taglish voice.

## Syllabus Coverage
- **Carbohydrates**: Monosaccharides (glucose, fructose), disaccharides (sucrose), polysaccharides (starch, cellulose), and metabolic pathways (glycolysis, gluconeogenesis).
- **Lipids**: Fatty acids, triglycerides, phospholipids, steroids, and membrane structure.
- **Nucleic Acids**: DNA/RNA structure, central dogma, replication/transcription, and genetic code.
- **Metabolism**: Krebs cycle, oxidative phosphorylation, and ATP production.

## Difficulty
- **Tier 1**: School-level recall and basic calculations.
- **Tier 2**: National olympiad-style applications.
- **Tier 3**: IChO-style multi-part reasoning.

## Required Problems
1. **Tier 1**: Compare glucose and fructose structures and functions.
2. **Tier 2**: Explain how glycolysis generates ATP and NADH.
3. **Tier 3**: Describe the role of ATP in metabolic regulation and energy storage.
