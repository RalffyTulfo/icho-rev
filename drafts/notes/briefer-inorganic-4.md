# Brief for Unit 3: Inorganic Chemistry, Lesson 4

## Title
Organometallics, Catalysis, and Solid-State Chemistry

## Goal
Teach students the 18-electron rule, synergic back-bonding in metal carbonyls, key catalytic cycles, and basic solid-state unit cell geometry.

## Syllabus Coverage
- **18-Electron Rule**: Counting valence electrons (Neutral/Covalent method preferred).
- **Metal Carbonyls**: Synergic $\pi$-backbonding and its effect on C-O IR stretching frequency ($\nu_{\text{CO}}$).
- **Catalysis**: Key steps (oxidative addition, reductive elimination, migratory insertion) and cycles (Wilkinson's, Monsanto/Cativa).
- **Solid-State**: Simple cubic, BCC, FCC, HCP lattices. Density calculations ($d = \frac{ZM}{V N_A}$). Bragg's Law.

## Difficulty
Advanced (IChO level).

## Format (Follow STYLE_GUIDE.md)
- **Voice**: Taglish ("Bakit sumusunod sa 18-electron rule ang metal?", "Solid state geometry is lowkey just Minecraft blocks").
- **Terms and Definitions box**: 18-electron rule, Hapticity ($\eta$), Back-bonding, Unit Cell, Coordination Number.
- **Worked Examples**:
    - Electron count for $\text{Fe(CO)}_5$ vs. $[\text{Mn(CO)}_6]^+$.
    - Predicting IR shifts for $\text{CO}$ vs $\text{CN}^-$.
    - Calculating density of a gold FCC unit cell.
- **Callouts**:
    - **Trap**: Forgetting to account for the charge in electron counting.
    - **Graders want**: Clear identification of catalytic steps.
- **2+ Interactives**:
    - **18-e- Counter**: Input a complex, get the count.
    - **Unit Cell Visualizer**: 3D viewer for BCC/FCC/HCP.

## Required Problems
1. **Warm-up**: Count electrons for Vaska's complex.
2. **Level-up**: Explain why $\nu_{\text{CO}}$ decreases as metal oxidation state decreases.
3. **Boss Fight**: Solve a solid-state structure given X-ray diffraction data and density.

## Constraints
- **QA**: Density calculations must match known physical constants ($N_A = 6.022 \times 10^{23} \text{ mol}^{-1}$).
