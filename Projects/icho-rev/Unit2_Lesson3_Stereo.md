# Stereochemistry and Conformational Analysis

Ano ba talaga ang nangyayari sa loob ng molekula? Hindi siya static na drawing sa papel. Gumagalaw 'yan, nag-aadjust, at 'yung shape niya, 'yun ang dikta ng lahat ng properties niya.

## Terms and Definitions

- **Chirality**: Ang pagiging "handedness" ng molekula. Kapag ang image mo sa salamin ay hindi mo na maipatong nang sakto sa sarili mo.
- **Enantiomers**: Chiral pairs na mirror images ng isa't isa.
- **Diastereomers**: Stereoisomers na hindi mirror images.
- **Conformational Isomers**: Same connectivity, iba lang ang rotation sa bond. Hindi sila "magkaibang" compound, magkaibang "pose" lang.

## Why does this exist?

Kasi sa chemistry, shape is destiny. Isang maliit na rotation o pagbaligtad ng group, pwedeng maging lason ang gamot o kaya hindi mag-bind sa receptor. If you ignore this, you're just drawing cartoons, not chemistry.

## Derivations from First Principles

1. **Cahn-Ingold-Prelog (CIP) Priorities**:
   - Highest atomic number, highest priority.
   - Kung pareho, move to the next atom.
   - Treat double bonds as two single bonds to the same atom.

2. **Newman Projections**:
   - Eclipsed conformation (torsional strain: pinaka-unstable).
   - Staggered conformation (minimum strain).
   - Anti vs Gauche (para sa alkanes).

## Common IChO Traps

- **"Chiral center detection"**: Minsan may plane of symmetry ka kahit may chiral center (meso compounds). Double check always.
- **"Newman confusion"**: Nakakalimutan i-rotate 'yung back carbon.
- **"Fisher Projection flips"**: Isang swap, invert configuration. Dalawang swaps, maintain. Huwag mag-rotate ng 90 degrees, disaster 'yan.

## Worked Example

**Problem**: Draw the most stable conformation of 1,2-dichlorocyclohexane and explain why.

**Solution**:
1. Identify the substituents: Both are chlorine atoms.
2. Determine the preferred positions: Both chlorines prefer equatorial positions to minimize 1,3-diaxial interactions.
3. Draw the chair conformation with both chlorines equatorial.
4. Conclude that this conformation is the most stable due to reduced steric strain.

## What Graders Want to See

- Clear Newman projections with labeled bonds.
- Explicit labeling of axial and equatorial positions.
- Explanation of steric and torsional strain considerations.
