# Reaction Mechanisms (Unit 2, Lesson 4)

So, gng, tapos na tayo sa basics. Alam na natin kung ano yung functional groups at stereochem. Ngayon, pag-usapan naman natin yung "how" — yung dance of electrons na nagpapagalaw sa universe ng organic chemistry.

## Terms and Definitions
- **Nucleophile ($\text{Nu}^-$)**: Electron-rich species, mahilig mag-donate ng electrons (the giver).
- **Electrophile ($\text{E}^+$)**: Electron-poor species, naghahanap ng electrons (the taker).
- **Curly Arrow**: Ang visual language natin para ipakita ang movement ng pairs of electrons. Mula electron source, papunta sa electron sink.
- **Transition State**: The awkward, unstable peak of an energy diagram where bonds are half-broken/half-formed.
- **Intermediate**: A short-lived species (like a carbocation) that sits in a valley on the energy landscape.

## Why does this exist?
Bakit kailangan pa natin 'to? Kasi hindi sapat na alam mo "ano" yung product, kailangan alam mo "paano" siya nabuo. Kung alam mo yung mechanism, mahuhulaan mo yung side products, yung reactivity, at kung bakit minsan hindi lumalabas yung expected product mo. Sa IChO, ito yung bread and butter.

## The Dance: Curly Arrow Mechanics
Always remember: **Tail** (saan galing) $\to$ **Head** (saan pupunta).

1. **$\text{S}_\text{N}2$ (Substitution, Nucleophilic, Bimolecular)**: Concerted process. Sabay ang attack at departure.
2. **$\text{S}_\text{N}1$ (Substitution, Nucleophilic, Unimolecular)**: Step-by-step. Break muna (carbocation formation), tapos attack.

## Worked Example: $\text{S}_\text{N}2$ Attack of $\text{OH}^-$ on $\text{CH}_3\text{Br}$

**Step 0: Pathing.**
$\text{OH}^-$ is a strong nucleophile. $\text{CH}_3\text{Br}$ has a polar bond ($\text{C}^{\delta+} - \text{Br}^{\delta-}$). Backside attack needed due to steric geometry.

**Reaction:**
$\text{HO}^- + \text{CH}_3\text{Br} \to \text{CH}_3\text{OH} + \text{Br}^-$

**Curly Arrow:**
Draw an arrow from the lone pair on $\text{O}$ of $\text{HO}^-$ to the central $\text{C}$.
Simultaneously, draw an arrow from the $\text{C}-\text{Br}$ bond to the $\text{Br}$ atom.

*Check*: Is the carbon valence exceeded? No, the $\text{Br}$ leaves as the $\text{OH}$ enters. Atom count is balanced.

## Common IChO Traps
- **Ignoring Sterics**: Akala mo attack lang basta-basta, pero kung bulkier yung group, hindi tatagos yung nucleophile.
- **Carbocation Rearrangement**: Sa $\text{S}_\text{N}1$, laging i-check kung pwedeng mag-shift (hydride or alkyl shift) para maging mas stable yung carbocation (tertiary > secondary > primary).
- **Arrow direction**: Never draw from atom to atom; always from electron source to sink.
