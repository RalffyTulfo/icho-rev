## Derivation from First Principles

### Degree of Polymerization in Step-Growth Polymerization
For condensation polymerization, the Carothers equation:

$$X_n = \frac{1}{1-p}$$

where:
- $X_n$ = average degree of polymerization
- $p$ = extent of reaction (fraction of functional groups that have reacted)

**IChO application:** For polycondensation of $A_2 + B_2$ with 95% reaction extent:
$X_n = \frac{1}{0.95} = 1.053$, demonstrating rapid molecular weight growth near completion.

### Number-Average Molecular Weight
$$M_n = X_n \times M_0$$

where:
- $M_n$ = number-average molecular weight  
- $M_0$ = molecular weight of repeating unit

**Example:** For nylon-6,6 ($C_{12}H_{22}N_2O_2$), $M_0 = 226.3 \text{ g/mol}$, $X_n = 100$:
$M_n = 100 \times 226.3 = 22,630 \text{ g/mol}$

### Polymerization Rate Laws
**Addition polymerization (free radical):**
$$\frac{d[M]}{dt} = -k_p [M][R\bullet]$$

where:
- $[M]$ = monomer concentration
- $[R\bullet]$ = radical concentration
- $k_p$ = rate constant

**IChO tip:** Chain transfer and termination steps significantly affect molecular weight control.

### Common IChO Traps
1. **Molecular weight confusion:** Always specify whether it's $M_n$, $M_w$, or $M_p$
2. **Extent of reaction:** Remember $p$ is the **fraction reacted**, not remaining unreacted
3. **Molecular weight growth:** $M_n$ grows linearly, but $M_w$ grows exponentially near gel point
4. **Chain transfer:** Use the Mayo equation for chain transfer constants
