Three stacked sine curves for n = 1, 2, 3 in a box from x = 0 to x = L, each touching zero at both walls, with 0, 1 and 2 interior nodes marked as red dots. To the right, two energy ladders. The box ladder rises as n squared: the n = 1, 2, 3 levels sit at 1, 4 and 9 units, so the gaps are 3 and 5 units and grow. The hydrogen ladder, quoted from the 3D Coulomb solution, has levels at -13.6, -3.40, -1.51 and -0.85 eV, so the gaps are 10.2, 1.89 and 0.66 eV and shrink toward the 0 eV ionisation limit.
