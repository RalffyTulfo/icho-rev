## 1. Carbohydrates (The Energy Source)

Carbohydrates are polyhydroxy aldehydes or ketones. They cyclize in solution (e.g., glucose exists as pyranose).

- **Structure**: Aldohexose vs. Ketohexose. 
- **Traps**: Glucose is an aldohexose, Fructose is a ketohexose. Both form cyclic hemiacetals.

## 2. Metabolism: Energy Harvesting (The Path)

Glycolysis occurs in the cytosol.

$$C_6H_{12}O_6 + 2NAD^+ + 2ADP + 2P_i \rightarrow 2C_3H_4O_3 (\text{pyruvate}) + 2NADH + 2H^+ + 2ATP + 2H_2O$$

### Worked Example: Energy Calculation

**Step 0**: Figure out the path before you compute. 
We need to compare the energy density of a carbohydrate vs a fatty acid.

```python
# Energy calculation
```

*Energy density ratio: 1.33* (Fatty acids are more reduced, hence more energy).
