## Spectroscopy: Seeing the Invisible

Hindi lang "pang-visual" ang spectroscopy. It is how we know what's happening at the molecular level using light. Sa IChO, hindi ka lang tatanungin kung ano yung spectrum, tatanungin ka kung *bakit* yun yung nakikita mo.

## 1. The Beer-Lambert Law

Eto ang bread and butter ng quantitative UV-Vis.
$$A = \varepsilon b c$$
- $A$: Absorbance (unitless)
- $\varepsilon$: Molar absorptivity (L·mol⁻¹·cm⁻¹)
- $b$: Path length (usually 1 cm)
- $c$: Concentration (mol/L)

*Logic check:* Bakit absorbance, hindi transmittance? Dahil absorbance is linear to concentration, transmittance is logarithmic.

## 2. UV-Vis: Electronic Transitions

Molecules absorb energy to promote electrons.
- **$\sigma \to \sigma^*$**: High energy, usually in far-UV (not useful for standard lab).
- **$\pi \to \pi^*$ & $n \to \pi^*$**: These are our targets! They occur at longer wavelengths, making them accessible to our instruments.
- **Conjugation**: Habang humahaba yung conjugation, bumababa yung energy gap (HOMO-LUMO gap), kaya nag-shi-shift yung absorption to longer wavelengths (**Bathochromic shift** or "red shift").

## 3. IR Spectroscopy: Vibrational Modes

Hindi na 'to electronic; vibrational na. Think of bonds as springs (Hooke's Law).
- **Fingerprint Region (< 1500 cm⁻¹)**: Complex pattern unique to the molecule.
- **Functional Group Region (> 1500 cm⁻¹)**: Diagnostic peaks (e.g., C=O at ~1700 cm⁻¹).

*IChO Pro-tip:* Symmetry matters! If a molecule is perfectly symmetric (like $CO_2$ stretching), the dipole moment doesn't change, so it's **IR inactive**.

## 4. NMR: The Magnetic Pulse

We use a strong magnetic field to flip nuclear spins.
- **Chemical Shift ($\delta$)**: Affected by electron density (shielding). E.g., Protons near electronegative atoms (like O or Cl) are **deshielded** (downfield shift, higher ppm).
- **Coupling ($J$-coupling)**: Interaction with neighboring protons ($n+1$ rule).

## Quiz: Master the Light

1. **The Shift**: Bakit tumataas ang wavelength (red shift) habang dumadami ang double bonds (conjugation)?
   *Answer: Conjugation stabilizes the excited state relative to the ground state, decreasing the HOMO-LUMO energy gap.*

2. **The Symmetry**: Why is the symmetric stretch of $N_2$ inactive in IR?
   *Answer: No change in dipole moment during vibration.*
