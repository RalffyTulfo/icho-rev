# Practical Lab Skills: Laboratory Safety, Titrimetry, and Spectroscopy

## Terms & Definitions

- **MSDS / SDS**: Material Safety Data Sheet detailing chemical hazards, personal protective equipment (PPE), and emergency response.
- **Aseptic Technique**: Procedures used in laboratory settings to prevent contamination by micro-organisms.
- **Primary Standard**: A substance of high purity (>99.9%) used to prepare standard solutions directly by mass (e.g., potassium hydrogen phthalate, KHP).
- **Spectrophotometry**: Quantitative measurement of light absorption intensity as a function of wavelength.
- **Blank Solution**: Reference solution containing all components except the analyte, used to zero absorbance instruments.

## Why Does This Exist?

Laboratory safety and precision measurements are the bedrock of experimental chemistry. IChO practical examinations evaluate meticulous technique, error minimization, and correct operation of volumetric and spectroscopic instruments.

## Derivation from First Principles

### Standardization of Sodium Hydroxide Using KHP
Potassium hydrogen phthalate (KHP, KHC8H5O4, monoprotic acid, molar mass = 204.23 g/mol) is a primary standard used to standardize NaOH solutions:
KHP + NaOH -> KNaC8H4O4 + H2O
At equivalence point:
n_NaOH = n_KHP = m_KHP / M_KHP
Molarity of NaOH = m_KHP / (M_KHP * V_NaOH_in_L)

## Worked Example

## Step 0: Calculate Mass of Primary Standard Required
**Problem**: Calculate the mass of primary standard potassium hydrogen phthalate (KHP, molar mass = 204.23 g/mol) required to neutralize exactly 25.00 mL of an approximately 0.1000 M NaOH solution.

**Solution**:
1. **Moles of NaOH needed**:
   n_NaOH = 0.02500 L * 0.1000 M = 2.500e-3 mol.
2. **Moles of KHP required** (1:1 stoichiometry):
   n_KHP = 2.500e-3 mol.
3. **Mass of KHP**:
   m_KHP = n_KHP * M_KHP = (2.500e-3 mol) * (204.23 g/mol) = 0.5106 g.

**Sanity Check**: Weighing out approximately 0.51 g of KHP ensures a titration volume of around 25 mL with a 0.1 M buret, which is ideal for minimizing volumetric reading errors.

## Common IChO Traps

1. **Buret Rinsing**: Always rinse burets with the titrant solution and volumetric flasks with distilled water (never rinse volumetric flasks with the solution they will contain).
2. **Meniscus Reading**: Always read the bottom of the meniscus at eye level to prevent parallax error.

## Recall Cards
- **Recall Card 1**: Why is potassium hydrogen phthalate (KHP) an ideal primary standard?
  Answer: It is non-hygroscopic, stable in air, has a high molar mass, and is available in ultra-high purity (>99.9%).
- **Recall Card 2**: How should a buret be prepared prior to a titration?
  Answer: Washed with distilled water and rinsed twice with small portions of the titrant solution itself.
